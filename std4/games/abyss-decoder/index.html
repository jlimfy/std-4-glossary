      How to play

      The read

      A Chinese term, phrase or question appears in the beacon at the top. Its possible English meanings sink down the screen, each in its own lane. Steer under the right one and pulse.

      the reflection of light
      the refraction of light

      Controls

      ← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D fly · space pulse · L read aloud · P pause

      With Read aloud switched on, each new question is spoken as it appears — L or the speaker button repeats it.

      The sub flies in both directions — up and down as well as across. Rising meets a capsule sooner, which is worth more speed points and reaches a canister faster, but it also puts you where the debris is. Above the faint line the water stays clear: that band is left open so a capsule can always be read before it is in reach.

      On a phone: drag anywhere on the water to fly, and tap PULSE to fire. Dragging never fires — moving can't cost you a hull plate.

      The dive

      • Ten clean reads clears a depth. Only a first-time correct hit counts. Shoot a wrong meaning first and you can still take the right one for points — but it won't count, and the item slides back into the deck to return a few turns later.
      • Clearing a depth repairs one hull plate and pays 300 × depth.
      • There's no fixed length. You keep diving until the hull runs out.

      Hull — only two things cost a plate

      • Pulsing a wrong meaning
      • Letting the correct meaning sink past you

      Wrong answers drifting past cost nothing, so you're never punished for reading carefully. Debris costs you beam power, never a plate.

      Canisters

      Dropped by a destroyed capsule — guaranteed on every third correct in a row, otherwise about half the time. Swim into one to collect it.

      Debris fields & salvage

      Between depths, a debris field runs for 18 seconds. Rocks split large → two medium → two small, and the smaller pieces are worth more. A collision never costs a hull plate — it knocks your beam down a level and eats into the clear bonus.

      Salvage is banked at 1 per 20 points, in a purse separate from your score, and spent in the Dock.
