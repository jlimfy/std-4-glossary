The run
  • Identify ten to clear a level. There is no end — the levels keep coming until your shields run out.
  • Only a clean hit counts toward the ten. Shoot a wrong meaning first and you can still take the right one for points, but that item drops back into the deck and returns a few turns later.
  • Four shields. Two things cost one: shooting a wrong meaning, or letting the correct meaning fall past you.
  • Clearing a level repairs a shield and pays a bonus that grows with the level.
  • Every meaning falls in its own lane, so the right one is never hidden behind a wrong one — line up under it and fire.
  • Wrong meanings can be ignored entirely; let them drift off the bottom. There is no penalty for waiting.
  • Answer quickly for a speed bonus; three in a row starts a multiplier, up to ×5.
  • Each level falls faster than the last, from about 15 seconds an item down to about 6.
Upgrade pods — fly into them to collect
CCannon — up to three levels. Faster, wider fire. A wrong shot knocks it back one level.
+Shield — repairs one shield.
TSlow-mo — the answers drift at half speed for eight seconds.
XScan — burns away one wrong answer. The 50/50 lifeline.
★Bonus — 300 points.
Asteroid fields and credits
  • An asteroid field drifts through between levels. Big rocks break into smaller ones, and smaller ones are worth more. Hitting a rock never costs a shield — it knocks your cannon down a level and eats into the clear bonus.
  • Every run banks credits — one for every 20 points. Spend them in the Hangar on new ships and accessories. Credits are a separate purse, so buying things never lowers your score.
  • Everything missed is listed on the debrief with its correct meaning, and can be replayed or read as study cards.
